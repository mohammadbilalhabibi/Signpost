# Finkenhof Detterstrasse student residence

## Detterstraße - Finkenhof

The residential complex, which was reopened in 2016 after general renovation, provides 65 accommodations. 63 complete single apartments are equipped with a private bathroom and a private kitchen. Among them, four complete single apartments on the ground floor are equipped wheelchair-user-friendly. In the shared-living flat on the top floor, both tenants share a bathroom and a kitchen as well as a large roof terrace.

## Common rooms

As a highlight, the residential complex provides a common room with a bowling alley in the basement, which can be used together with tenants from the Frauenstraße.

## Further information

It is possible to do the laundry in two rooms with washing machines and dryers for collective use on the ground floor and in the basement of the house.

For bicycles, there are roofed outside parking lots. There are parking spaces for cars.

## Address

Detterstraße 21, D-94469 Deggendorf

## Rents

€ 362,- to € 466,- for a complete single apartment € 478,- to € 492,- for a shared-living flat for two occupants € 435,- to € 504,- for a complete single apartment, suitable for wheelchair users € 12,- for a car parking space (outside parking lot)
