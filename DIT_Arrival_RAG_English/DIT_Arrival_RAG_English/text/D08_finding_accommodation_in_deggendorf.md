# Finding accommodation in Deggendorf

## accommodation

## How to find an apartment

To help you find a flat or a room at one of our study locations, we have put together some tips and information on finding accommodation below. In Deggendorf, you can apply for a place in a student park; these usually offer single-room flats. In Pfarrkirchen, there is a new student park as well as private options. In Cham, there are currently no official student parks, but there are plenty of private options available for students.

## Tips for your apartment search

- Start looking early as flat vacancies are often posted 3 months ahead of move-in date

- Students often live in shared flats, known as ‘WGs’ for short. WGs are a great way to get to know other students

- Have a look on flat rental websites and social media. Use hashtags like Wohnungen, WG or WG-Zimmer, and the town where you are going to study

- Check whether your intended study programme is based in Deggendorf, Pfarrkirchen or Cham and specify your search to the town where your studies are located

- Be aware that every household in Germany has to pay the TV and radio licence fee

[TV and radio licence fee](https://www.rundfunkbeitrag.de/welcome/englisch/index_ger.html)

- View the living costs chart to see a detailed breakdown of expenses you will be required to pay during studies

[living costs chart](https://th-deg.de/en/study-with-us/funding)

## Learn the lingo

- Wohnung = flat

- WG = shared flat

- WG-Zimmer = room in a shared flat

- NK = utility costs

- KM = basic rent without utilities

- WM = rent with utilities

## student accommodation in deggendorf

In Deggendorf, the majority of international students live in student parks, which are all located centrally within easy walking distance of campus and the town centre. These privately owned student parks are modern, affordabe, and attractive low level apartment blocks consisting of compact, furnished and self-contained flats mainly for single occupation, although other options are available. Most dorms have a communal lounge, laundry room, wifi, parking spaces and a bike lock-up. In order to secure one of these affordable apartments, we recommend that you apply directly over their websites (below) at the earliest date possible.
