# Untere Himmelreichstrasse student residence

## Untere Himmelreichstraße

The new residential complex is located in the immediate vicinity of the already existing residential complexes in Detterstraße and Frauenstraße (Finkenhof). The way to the university is short: the TH Deggendorf (DIT) is about two kilometers away.

The complex consists of 61 living units on three floors. It contains 61 single full apartments (with their own bathroom and kitchen), three communal kitchens and a communal toilet. The apartments are each equipped with a bed, desk, wardrobe, kitchenette and curtains.

## Common rooms

There is a shared kitchen and toilet on the ground floor. The kitchen can be reached barrier-free. The toilet, which is located in the immediate vicinity of the barrier-free entrance, is also designed to be barrier-free. Further communal kitchens are located on the 1st and 2nd floors. Outside there is a barbecue area.

## Further information

There is a laundry lounge with washing machines and dryers on the ground floor. Car parking spaces and covered bicycle parking spaces are available in the outdoor area of the residential complex. Short distances, a good connection to the train station - the city park and the city center can be reached quickly - offer perfect conditions for comfortable living close to the university.
