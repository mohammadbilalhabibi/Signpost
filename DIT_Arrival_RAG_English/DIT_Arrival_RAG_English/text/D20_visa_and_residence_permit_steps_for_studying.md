# Visa and residence-permit steps for studying

At a Glance: Visa for Studying

Visa and entry procedure for immigrants from third countries subject to visa obligation. General entitlement requirements (Section 5 of the Residence Act [AufenthG]): passport, proof of financial means, no existing reason for expulsion.

## Step 1 - Check The Requirements

 • Admission to study in Germany (if applicable, admission to preparatory course).  • Foreign or German school-leaving certificate with university entrance qualification (in the case of doctorate and master's degree: corresponding recognised academic degree).  • Secure financial means (scholarship, blocked account with at least €11,904 (as of 2026) or Declaration of Commitment).  • Proof of language skills, if required.

## Step 2 - Submit The Digital Visa Application

Create a user account on the Consular Services Portal and apply for your visa online. Prepare the required documents: e.g. passport, university entrance qualification, confirmation of university admission or confirmation of attendance at a preparatory course, proof of financial means, proof of language skills (if required).

Please consider: if online visa applications are not yet available in your country, please contact the competent German mission abroad for further information on the visa procedure.

## Step 3 - Make An Appointment At The German Embassy

 • Bring the original documents in their entirety.  • Pay the visa fees: €75 (in local currency).

Please consider: there may be waiting times when requesting an appointment. Processing times may strongly vary depending on the mission abroad and processing workload. Please refer to the competent embassy for further information.

## Step 4 - Enter Germany

 • Issuance of the visa for studying.  • Book flight / plan journey to Germany.

Please consider: in order for an entry visa to be issued, proof of valid health insurance coverage must be presented. After entering Germany, new health insurance must be taken out.

## Step 5 - Apply For A Residence Permit In Germany

 • Register your home address in Germany at the Residents' Registration Office.  • Book an appointment at the competent foreigners authority.  • Ask the foreigners authority for a list of the required documents and start the preparation.  • Apply for the residence permit for studying (Section 16b of the Residence Act [AufenthG]).  • Fees may add up to €100 (Sections 45 ff. of the Residence Ordinance [AufenthV]).

Please consider: the application for the residence permit must be submitted before the entry visa expires.

This overview is a simplified representation of the visa procedure. The steps described are solely for the purpose of clarifying the application procedure for residence permits. Further details on the visa application procedure and information on important points of contact can be found at www.make-it-in-germany.com.
