# Student residence-permit document checklist

## Required documents - English checklist on the authority website

- Valid passport incl. visa and entry stamp if applicable (copy)

- Recent biometric photograph

- Tenancy agreement (copy)

- proof of sufficient funds to cover living expenses (if applicable, through a blocked account or official declaration of commitment)

- University enrolment certificate and certificate of study time issued by the university

- University admission notification (copy)

- Health insurance certificate

- Fee €100

## Additional instructions - English translation prepared for this collection

The German checklist specifies a biometric photograph with a QR code.

DIT students should email the residence-permit application and required documents to the contact responsible for their surname letter. Include your name, date of birth and address. Consult the current contact list on the source page.

After processing the application, the immigration office contacts you to arrange an appointment. Visits are by appointment only. The office asks applicants to refrain from enquiries about processing status.
