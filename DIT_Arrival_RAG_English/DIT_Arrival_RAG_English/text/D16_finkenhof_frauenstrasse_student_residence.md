# Finkenhof Frauenstrasse student residence

## Frauenstraße - Finkenhof

The residential complex provides 28 dwelling places. Of these, 24 complete single apartments are equipped with a private bathroom and a private kitchen. In the shared-living flats for four occupants in the basement, the four tenants share two bathrooms and one kitchen.

## Common rooms

As a highlight, the residential complex provides a common room with a bowling alley in the basement of the Detterstraße, which can be used together with the tenants of the Detterstraße.

## Further information

It is possible to do the laundry in two rooms with washing machines and dryers for collective use on the ground floor and in the basement of the Detterstraße.

There are roofed outside parking lots for bicycles. There are parking spaces for cars.

## Address

Frauenstraße 46, D-94469 Deggendorf

## Rents

315,- € to 354,- € for a complete single apartment 369,- € to 397,- € for an apartment in the shared living flat for four occupants
