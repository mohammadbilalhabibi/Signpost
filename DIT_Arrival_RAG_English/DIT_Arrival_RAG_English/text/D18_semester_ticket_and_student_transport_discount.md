# Semester ticket and student transport discount

## Semester ticket Deggendorf

At the moment there is no semester ticket agreement for TH Deggendorf students.

## Bavarian discount ticket and "Aufpreisticket" for students

As there is no semester ticket agreement for students at TH Deggendorf, students enrolled at TH Deggendorf cannot purchase a surcharge ticket for the semester ticket. However, it is still possible to purchase the discounted ticket for students. The sales partner is VAG Verkehrs-Aktiengesellschaft (VAG, Nuremberg) . Tickets can be purchased via the VAG subscription portal.

[VAG Verkehrs-Aktiengesellschaft (VAG, Nuremberg)](https://www.vag.de/en/)
