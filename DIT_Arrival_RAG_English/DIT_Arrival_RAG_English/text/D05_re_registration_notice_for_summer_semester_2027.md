## Original PDF page 1

Seite 3 von 4

ANNOUNCEMENT

Re-registration for the summer semester 2027

(via PayPal or credit card)

Re-registration for the summer semester 2027 must be carried out

between

Friday, 08 January 2027 and Friday, 05 February 2027

by paying the following fees and charges directly in the Primuss portal via

PayPal or credit card (Visa or Mastercard). You will find a button for this in

your online portal.

• Student union fee amounting to € 87,--

• Service fee amounting to € 500,--

only applies to students who are not EU citizens, do not have a

German university entrance qualification and were first matriculated

in the winter semester 2023/24 or later (after the relevant fee

regulations came into force on 15 April 2023)

For details:

https://www.th-deg.de/Studierende/Antraege-und-Organisatorisches/Hochschulrecht/gebuehrensatzung_bes_aufwendung.pdf

In accordance with Art. 93 para. 1 of the Bavarian Higher Education

Innovation Act in conjunction with Section 1 2 of the Matriculation, Re -

registration and De- registration Statutes of Deggendorf Institute of

Technology, every student must register for their continuation of studies

in due form and time.

Signed

Centre for Studies posted on 01/10/2026

Notes:

As soon as the payment data has been transmitted by the

Staatsoberkasse and entered into the database, the registration certificate

can be printed via the Primuss portal.

The enrolment certificate for the coming semester will be available

on the Primuss portal at the beginning of the semester.

Please note that if you are enrolled in two degree programmes, the

student union fee of 87 euros is only payable for one programme.
