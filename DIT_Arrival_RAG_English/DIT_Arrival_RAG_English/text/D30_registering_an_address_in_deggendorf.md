# Registering an address in Deggendorf

## English summary of the city registration instructions

After moving into accommodation in Deggendorf, register your address within two weeks. The same deadline applies when you move within the city. Registration can take place from the actual move-in date.

- Bring your identity card and/or passport.

- Obtain the housing provider’s confirmation of your move-in (Wohnungsgeberbestätigung). The housing provider or an authorised representative supplies it.

- The city describes registration in person at the Bürgeramt. An authorised representative can act for you. The office normally prepares the registration form for signature.

The city page links to the housing-provider confirmation form and online appointment booking. Use those links for the current forms and available appointments.
