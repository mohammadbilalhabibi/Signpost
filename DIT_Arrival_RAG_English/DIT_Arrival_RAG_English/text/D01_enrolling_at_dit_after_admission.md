# Enrolling at DIT after admission

## How to enrol

Have you received a letter of admission?

If you are admitted to a degree programme, you must log in to the Primuss portal online and upload a passport photo. You must then confirm your application for enrollment. You will also need enrollment documents, consisting of various applications and forms, which can be found in your letter of admission. On this letter you will also find the enrollment deadlines and all further steps. If you do not meet this deadline, your admission will be cancelled.

Enrollment

In the letter of admission email, we will tell you which documents must be uploaded for enrollment. In general, your list will contain a request for the following documents unless other items are requested in the letter of admission:

- Application of Enrollment

[Application of Enrollment](https://www.th-deg.de/Studierende/Antraege-und-Organisatorisches/Antraege/Antrag_Immatrikulation_en.pdf)

- Copy of a valid identity card or passport

- Officially certified copy of the higher education entrance qualification

- Curriculum vitae

[Curriculum vitae](https://th-deg.de/Studierende/Antraege-und-Organisatorisches/Antraege/Lebenslauf.pdf)

- Language Agreement

[Language Agreement](https://www.th-deg.de/Studierende/Antraege-und-Organisatorisches/German%20Language%20Requirement_ECRI_EN.pdf)

- Language Requirements (language of instruction)

[Language Requirements (language of instruction)](https://www.th-deg.de/Studierende/Antraege-und-Organisatorisches/Antraege/Sprachanforderungen_Studiengangssprache_EN.pdf)

- Tuberculosis report form

[Tuberculosis report form](https://th-deg.de/Studierende/Antraege-und-Organisatorisches/Antraege/Information_Tuberculosis.pdf)

- Power of attorney with your original signature, if applicable

- Proof of completed internship, if applicable

- Confirmation of de-registration from another university, if applicable

- Form for parents of u18 minors , if applicable

[Form for parents of u18 minors](https://th-deg.de/Studierende/Antraege-und-Organisatorisches/Antraege/minderjaehrige.pdf)

- Guidance interview for the professionally qualified , if applicable

[Guidance interview for the professionally qualified](https://www.th-deg.de/Studierende/Antraege-und-Organisatorisches/Antraege/Guidance_interview_professionally_qualified.pdf)

Be aware that you are given an enrollment deadline, which will vary depending on the course. You must completely upload all documents before the end of your deadline otherwise your enrollment will become invalid. Please check regularly on the Primuss portal to see your enrollment status.

Please also note our information regarding health insurance.

[health insurance.](https://th-deg.de/Studierende/Antraege-und-Organisatorisches/Antraege/healt-insurance.pdf)

In order to complete your enrollment at DIT, the student union fee and any service fees for international students from non-EU countries must be paid to the account of the Staatsoberkasse Bayern within the deadline stated in the letter of admission. Payment is made directly from the Primuss applicant portal via PayPal or credit card. You will find a button for this in the online portal .

[online portal](http://www3.primuss.de/cgi-bin/bew_anmeldung_v2/index.pl?FH=thd)
