## Student ID card

When you enrol at Deggendorf Institute of Technology, you will receive your student ID card. This not only allows you to officially identify yourself as a student and take advantage of student discounts in shops, for example, but also gives you access to many of the university's services: for example, you can use it to borrow books from the library, pay in the canteen and copy shop or use the student car park.

About a week before the start of the semester, we will send the student ID card by post to newly enrolled students with a German mailing address - to the address you provided when you enrolled. If you move earlier, please set up a forwarding order in good time.

If you are studying at the Pfarrkirchen campus (ECRI) or have given an address abroad, you can pick up your student ID card in person at the Centre for Studies from the start of the semester.

Validation

When your student ID card is validated at the terminal, the current semester dates are printed on it and all functions are activated. You must repeat this process at the beginning of each semester. Your card will usually arrive already validated - so you can use it straight away.

Payment function

To be able to pay with your student card, you must first top it up. You will find top-up machines at the entrances to the canteen and cafeterias.

You can find information and help about your student ID card here: Student ID card

[Student ID card](https://www.th-deg.de/en/students/documents#student-id-card)

## Housing & living

We recommend moving near the campus. New students in particular should complete their semester close to campus in order to benefit from the campus facilities and the numerous events for students. You can also make contacts better on campus and get to know the university.

You can find information on finding accommodation on this page: Accommodation

If you can't find a flat in the immediate vicinity of the campus, we have put together some helpful tips on how to get to the campus quickly and easily: Mobility

Students can purchase the Deutschlandticket (German public transport system for 38€ per month) at a reduced price, you can find more information here: Deutschlandticket for students

## Financing & re-registration

It is best to clarify the financing of your studies before you start. Get information here and find the right funding or a suitable scholarship for you: Financing

After the first semester, you must re-register for each subsequent semester in which you wish to continue studying. To do this, you must transfer the student union fee and any service fees for the next semester by a set deadline. You can find the exact dates and information on the semester fee and payment here: Re-registration

As soon as the re-registration for the next semester is due, we will also inform you again by email, on my.th-deg.de and via our social media channels.
