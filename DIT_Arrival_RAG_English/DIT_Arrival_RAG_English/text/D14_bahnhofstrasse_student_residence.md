# Bahnhofstrasse student residence

## Bahnhofstraße

The student housing complex is located in the immediate vicinity of the city center and the way to the university is also short: the campus of the DIT is only about one kilometer away. There are a total of 83 student apartments in the complex - 31 apartments in house 55 and 52 apartments in house 55a. There are 79 single flats, which are equipped with their own kitchenette and bathroom. Four places are divided between two 2-person shared flats. All living spaces are equipped with a bed including mattress, desk, chair, wardrobe, mobile pedestal, lamps and curtains. The kitchen is equipped with a cooker, fridge with freezer compartment and sink. An internet connection is also available.

## Common rooms

There is a common room for residents in the basement. It is equipped with chairs and tables.

## Further information

There are parking spaces for bicycles in the basement as well as outside. The laundry lounge with washing machines and dryers is also in the basement. Car parking spaces are available outside and in a separate underground garage.

## Address

Bahnhofstraße 55-55a, D-94469 Deggendorf

## Rents

€ 306,05 to € 454,- for a complete single apartment (depending on the size) € 359,54 to € 360,14 for a shared-living flat for two occupants
